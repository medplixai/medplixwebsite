We already use another software in our Mumbai clinic — can you migrate our data?

In most cases, yes, and the migration is handled remotely. You share an export from your current system, and our team maps your patients, inventory and billing records into Medplix before go-live. If something cannot be carried over cleanly, we tell you upfront rather than after you have switched.

Yes. Medplix is cloud software, so staff at each branch log into the same account from a browser and see the same live data, wherever the branch is. There is nothing to install on-site and no server to maintain at any location.
