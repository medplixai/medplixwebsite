Why we exist

Built from inside Indian healthcare — not from the outside

Medplix comes from a team that grew up around Indian healthcare — pharmacy counters, medicine distribution and clinics — and lived the daily reality of paper registers, missing case sheets and software that nobody on the staff could actually use. Most healthcare software is built in big-city offices for big-city hospitals. We build for the front desk in Guntur, the pharmacy in Ongole and the lab in Karimnagar just as much as for a multi-branch chain in Hyderabad.

That is why everything about Medplix is shaped around how Indian healthcare actually runs: GST billing out of the box, prices in plain rupees on the website, remote onboarding so no one waits for an engineer to visit, and a team that trains your staff in Telugu — the language they actually work in.
